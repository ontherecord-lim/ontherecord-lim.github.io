## Article filing checklist

- Record article publication date, final headline, article ID and publisher URL.
- Check the original web page and byline. The portfolio catalogue was populated from saved site metadata; some records may need editorial verification.
- Save a personal PDF from the original publisher page in your browser (Print → Save as PDF) and mark its status in the catalogue. **Original article PDFs are not included in this repository.** Do not publish full-text publisher PDFs to a public GitHub repository without confirming reuse rights.
- Save a cover screenshot for the 10 selected pieces if needed for job applications.
- Add reporting type, original/interview status, and your contribution manually after checking notes or published text.
